── 5. NORMATIVE (in scope) ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

@sys.term.playable-day/1            term        active   scope all
  One in-game day, from the morning wake state to the following morning wake
  state, played end to end by one player without a crash, a soft-lock, or a
  placeholder asset.
  #day-boundary  A day boundary is the morning wake state, not midnight.
                 Sleeping is what ends a day; the clock is not.

@sys.term.tandem-work/1             term        active   scope all
  An engine change and its matching simulator change, landed in one commit. Work
  is in tandem when neither half is observable in the repository without the
  other.

@sys.constraint.single-threaded-sim/1 constraint  active   scope path "sim/**"
  The simulator is single-threaded. The determinism requirement and the target
  platform's threading model leave no room for a work-stealing scheduler in the
  tick loop.

@sys.policy.tandem-work/1           policy      active   scope all  [AT RISK depth 2]
  No engine change lands without the matching simulator change in the same
  commit, except on the tracks listed under exceptions, where the simulator may
  lag by at most one milestone.
  #lag-bound    Permitted simulator lag on an excepted track is at most one
                milestone, never two.
  #same-commit  Matching engine and simulator changes ship in one commit, not
                in a follow-up commit on the same day.
  supported_by  @sys.assessment.projection-gaps/1  [AT RISK depth 1]
  exceptions    @sys.track.lighting/1

@sys.req.deterministic-sim/1        requirement active   scope path "sim/**"
  Given the same seed and the same input sequence, two runs of the simulator
  produce byte-identical state at every tick boundary, on any supported
  platform.

@lege.term.renderer-boundary/1      term        active   via depends_on, depth 2
  The line between the viewer and the simulator. The viewer may read a frame
  snapshot across it; it may not call across it, and no engine type may appear
  in a viewer signature.

@sys.constraint.frame-budget-16ms/1 constraint  active   via depends_on, depth 2
  The target hardware runs at 60 Hz. A frame that takes longer than 16 ms drops,
  and dropped frames in the day loop read as a broken game rather than a slow
  one.
